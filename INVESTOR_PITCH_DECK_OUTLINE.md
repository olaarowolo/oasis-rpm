# Investor Pitch Deck Outline

## 1. Title Slide

- TheOAsis Research Supervision Portal
- Research operations infrastructure for Nigerian universities
- Tagline: digitize supervision, approvals, reporting, and archives across the full academic workflow

## 2. Problem

Nigerian universities still run major parts of postgraduate and project supervision through fragmented spreadsheets, email threads, paper forms, and informal follow-up.

Core pain points:

- slow proposal and approval cycles
- poor visibility into supervisor and student progress
- weak audit trail for academic decisions
- difficult senate, accreditation, and management reporting
- incomplete or inaccessible institutional archives

## 3. Why Now

- universities are under pressure to improve operational efficiency
- postgraduate and research workflows are growing in volume and complexity
- leadership increasingly expects digital reporting and traceability
- AI and analytics create a credible step-change in academic operations productivity

## 4. Solution

TheOAsis is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that manages the research supervision lifecycle across students, supervisors, admins, and institutional leadership.

Core capabilities:

- role-based student, supervisor, admin, and super-admin workflows
- proposal submission, review, approval, and revision handling
- meeting logs, stage tracking, and progress visibility
- resource approval and archive submission workflows
- analytics, audit logs, and institutional reporting
- configurable multi-campus and multi-university administration

## 5. Product Walkthrough

Show the product as an academic operations system rather than a simple portal.

Demo flow:

- student proposal submission
- supervisor review and feedback
- admin oversight and configuration
- dashboard and analytics outputs
- archive and institutional reporting workflow

## 6. Market Opportunity

Primary market:

- Nigerian universities
- polytechnics and colleges with project supervision requirements
- postgraduate schools and faculty-level deployments

Expansion path:

- institution-wide rollout
- multi-campus groups
- adjacent African higher-education markets

## 7. Business Model

Revenue layers:

- annual campus subscription
- one-time implementation and onboarding
- premium add-ons for AI, reporting, integrations, SMS, and dedicated hosting

Reference pricing:

- Essential: ₦6,000,000 annual subscription
- Professional: ₦12,000,000 annual subscription
- Enterprise: ₦24,000,000 annual subscription
- average implementation: ₦3,500,000 per campus
- blended first-year value per campus: ₦15,300,000

## 8. Go-To-Market

Sales motion:

- target postgraduate schools, deans, ICT directors, and academic leadership
- start with paid pilots in one faculty or school
- prove reduction in approval delays and admin workload
- expand into institution-wide licenses and upsell analytics, AI, and integrations

Q1 wedge:

- pitch deck
- ROI calculator
- pilot package
- buyer list
- target 20 to 30 qualified institutional conversations

## 9. Competitive Positioning

Position against:

- manual and spreadsheet-driven workflows
- generic LMS or student information tools not built for supervision governance
- internal custom tools with limited audit, analytics, or tenant management

Differentiators:

- built specifically for research supervision operations
- multi-tenant university model
- role-specific workflows
- audit and analytics visibility
- modular commercial model suited to Nigerian procurement

## 10. Traction and Proof Plan

Near-term traction goals:

- 2 to 4 paid pilots
- 5 to 10 signed annual subscriptions after pilot conversion
- first reference campuses and case studies

Pilot success metrics:

- proposal approval turnaround time reduction
- improvement in supervision meeting compliance
- archive submission completion rate
- admin hours saved

Scenario view:

- 50 campuses: ₦765,000,000 full contract value

Recurring base after implementation:

- 10 campuses: ₦118,000,000 annual recurring base
- 25 campuses: ₦295,000,000 annual recurring base
- 50 campuses: ₦590,000,000 annual recurring base

## 12. The Ask

Possible investor ask structure:

- fund GTM execution and campus acquisition
- finance implementation capacity and customer success
- support product hardening, integrations, and analytics expansion

Use of funds:

- sales and business development
