# Q1 GTM Execution Plan

## Q1 Goal

Build the core commercial assets and generate 20 to 30 qualified institutional conversations with Nigerian universities.

## Q1 Deliverables

1. Investor-style pitch deck outline
2. Client-facing pricing brochure
3. ROI calculator template
4. Paid pilot package
5. Buyer list template and target account list
6. Monthly financial model with invoice and cash collection timing

## Definition of a Qualified Institutional Conversation

A conversation counts as qualified if:

- the institution is within the target segment
- at least one operational or economic stakeholder participates
- there is a defined supervision, postgraduate, archive, or reporting pain point
- there is a plausible path to pilot, budget discussion, or internal sponsorship

## Weekly Execution Plan

### Weeks 1 to 2

- finalize positioning and messaging
- adapt pitch deck for investor and client use
- finalize brochure, pricing, and ROI calculator
- define pilot offer and baseline metrics

Target outcome:

- all core GTM assets completed

### Weeks 3 to 4

- build target account list
- source decision-makers and champions
- segment accounts into private, state, and federal opportunities
- prioritize accounts with active postgraduate or project-heavy programs

Target outcome:

- 50 to 75 target accounts with named stakeholders

### Weeks 5 to 8

- run outbound email and LinkedIn outreach
- request warm introductions through academic and ICT networks
- book discovery meetings and product demos
- qualify institutions using the ROI and pilot framework

Target outcome:

- 12 to 18 qualified institutional conversations

### Weeks 9 to 12

- deepen active opportunities
- deliver tailored demos and pilot proposals
- refine pricing and objections handling
- move best-fit institutions into pilot negotiation

Target outcome:

- 20 to 30 qualified institutional conversations total
- 5 to 8 strong pilot candidates

## Channel Plan

- direct outreach to postgraduate deans and ICT directors
- warm introductions through academic contacts
- conference and association networking
- targeted follow-up with faculties showing project supervision pain
- repository and archive modernization conversations with librarians

## KPIs

- number of target accounts built
- number of contacts sourced
- outreach response rate
- meetings booked
- qualified conversations completed
- pilot proposals sent
- pilot candidates in active negotiation

## Operating Cadence

- weekly pipeline review
- weekly messaging refinement based on objections
- biweekly update of buyer list and target segments
- monthly review of conversion from first contact to qualified conversation

## Q1 Exit Criteria

Q1 is successful if the team finishes with:

- complete GTM asset pack
- 20 to 30 qualified institutional conversations
- at least 5 serious pilot opportunities
- clear evidence of best-fit segment and strongest buyer persona
