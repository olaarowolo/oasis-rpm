# Nigeria University SaaS Pricing, GTM, and Revenue Model

## Product Positioning

TheOAsis Research Supervision Portal should be sold as institutional research operations infrastructure for Nigerian universities, not as a direct-to-student app.

The commercial value proposition is:

- reduce proposal and approval delays
- improve supervisor accountability and visibility
- digitize postgraduate and project workflow records
- create a searchable institutional archive
- support audit, quality assurance, and senate reporting

Primary buyers:

- Dean, School of Postgraduate Studies
- DVC Academic or DVC Research
- Director of ICT
- Registry or Academic Planning
- University Librarian for archive and repository use cases

## Recommended Business Model

The recommended model is B2B multi-tenant SaaS with three revenue layers:

1. Annual campus subscription
2. One-time implementation and onboarding fee
3. Optional premium modules, integrations, and support

This fits Nigerian university procurement better than pure per-seat pricing because budgets are typically approved at school, faculty, directorate, or institution level.

## GTM Plan for Nigerian Universities

### 1. Beachhead Market

Start with institutions where the operational pain is visible and decision chains are short enough to close.

Best initial segments:

- private universities with active postgraduate programs
- state universities modernizing postgraduate administration
- faculties with heavy final-year project supervision load
- universities building or cleaning up digital repositories

Avoid leading with very large federal universities first unless there is a strong internal sponsor.

### 2. Wedge Offer

Lead with one clear promise:

"Reduce postgraduate and project supervision bottlenecks while creating a complete digital audit trail."

The strongest initial deployment offers are:

- School of Postgraduate Studies workflow digitization
- Faculty-level final-year project supervision management
- Thesis and project archive digitization with approval workflow

### 3. Buyer Map

Economic buyers:

- Vice Chancellor
- DVC Academic or Research
- bursary or procurement approval chain

Operational buyers:

- postgraduate dean
- faculty dean
- registry
- librarian
- director of ICT

Internal champions:

- postgraduate coordinators
- faculty officers
- ICT system administrators
- supervisors with large student rosters

### 4. Sales Motion

Use a land-and-expand motion.

Stage 1: Discovery

- map current manual supervision, approval, and archive process
- quantify pain in turnaround time, missing records, and reporting gaps
- identify one sponsor with authority and one operational champion

Stage 2: Paid pilot

- deploy for one school, faculty, or postgraduate office
- migrate a limited dataset
- configure roles, approval flow, branding, and reports
- train admins, supervisors, and student reps

Stage 3: Prove value

Track pilot metrics such as:

- proposal approval turnaround time
- percentage of students with current supervision status
- number of completed meeting logs
- archive submission completion rate
- admin hours saved per cycle

Stage 4: Expand

- convert pilot to annual institutional agreement
- roll out to more faculties or programs
- add analytics, AI, SMS, and integrations

### 5. Procurement Strategy

University deals in Nigeria often slow down because the product is framed too narrowly. Frame it as digital academic operations infrastructure.

Use these procurement-friendly claims:

- improves institutional record keeping
- strengthens quality assurance and audit readiness
- supports postgraduate administration efficiency
- reduces paper-based workflow delays
- provides management reporting for senate and leadership

Recommended contracting options:

- faculty-level annual license
- postgraduate-school annual license
- institution-wide annual license
- implementation plus annual support agreement

### 6. Demand Generation Channels

Prioritize direct and relationship-led channels over broad self-serve marketing.

Best channels:

- warm introductions through university administrators and academics
- demos to postgraduate schools and ICT directorates
- conference presence at higher education and academic administration events
- case studies from pilot campuses
- targeted LinkedIn and email outreach to senior university decision-makers
- partnerships with university ICT consultants and repository vendors

### 7. Messaging Framework

For leadership:

- visibility, compliance, institutional control, reporting

For postgraduate offices and faculties:

- faster approvals, less manual follow-up, cleaner records

For ICT:

- secure multi-tenant platform, audit trail, configurable roles, reduced shadow processes

For supervisors:

- easier student tracking, meeting logs, proposal review, and progress visibility

### 8. 12-Month GTM Sequence

| Quarter | Focus | Target Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Build pitch deck, ROI calculator, pilot package, buyer list | 20 to 30 qualified institutional conversations |
| Q2 | Close 2 to 4 paid pilots | First reference customers and case studies |
| Q3 | Convert pilots and expand inside winning campuses | 5 to 10 signed annual subscriptions |
| Q4 | Formalize partner motion and enterprise upsells | Strong renewal pipeline and larger campus deals |

## Financial Model

### Base Assumptions

The model below uses a blended sales mix suitable for the first scale phase.

- 50% of campuses buy Essential at ₦6,000,000 per year
- 35% of campuses buy Professional at ₦12,000,000 per year
- 15% of campuses buy Enterprise at ₦24,000,000 per year
- blended annual subscription per campus = ₦10,800,000
- average one-time implementation fee per campus = ₦3,500,000
- 40% of campuses buy add-ons averaging ₦2,500,000 each
- blended add-on revenue per campus = ₦1,000,000

From these assumptions:

- first-year revenue per campus = ₦15,300,000
- recurring revenue per campus after year one, before renewals and expansion = ₦11,800,000

### Cash Planning Notes

- Collections may be uneven due to university procurement and budget release cycles.
- Annual upfront invoicing is better than monthly billing for this market.
- Implementation fees improve cash flow and reduce onboarding risk.
- Multi-campus or system-wide contracts should include phased rollout payments.

## Recommended Commercial Targets

Suggested near-term targets:

- Year 1 target: 5 to 10 campuses
- Year 2 target: 15 to 25 campuses
- Year 3 target: 30 to 50 campuses

Key commercial metrics to track:

- pilot-to-paid conversion rate
- average contract value
- implementation margin
- add-on attach rate
- renewal rate
- time from first meeting to contract signature

## Summary Recommendation

The most practical model for this product is:

- annual institutional license as the core offer
- paid implementation as a standard part of every deal
- premium modules and integrations as expansion revenue
- pilot-first GTM to build proof in Nigerian universities

This model matches both the product's workflow depth and the realities of higher-education procurement in Nigeria.
