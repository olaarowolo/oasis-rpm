# Investor Pitch Deck Slide Copy

## Slide 1: Title

**TheOAsis Research Supervision Portal**

Research operations infrastructure for Nigerian universities.

Digitize supervision, approvals, reporting, and archives across the full student research lifecycle.

## Slide 2: The Problem

Universities still manage critical research supervision workflows through fragmented spreadsheets, email threads, paper forms, and manual follow-up.

- proposal and topic approvals move slowly
- supervisors and admins lack real-time visibility
- academic decisions are hard to audit and report
- archives are incomplete, scattered, or difficult to search

The result is delayed student progress, higher admin workload, and weak institutional control.

## Slide 3: Why This Matters

Research supervision is not a side workflow. It directly affects postgraduate throughput, student experience, academic quality, and institutional credibility.

When universities cannot track supervision cleanly, they struggle to:

- monitor student progress at scale
- enforce accountability across supervisors and units
- produce reliable reports for leadership and senate
- maintain usable institutional knowledge and archives

## Slide 4: The Opportunity

Nigerian universities are increasingly under pressure to modernize academic operations, improve quality assurance, and build stronger digital records.

This creates a clear opening for category-defining software focused on research operations rather than generic LMS or admin tooling.

TheOAsis is positioned to become the operating system for supervision and project governance across universities.

## Slide 5: The Solution

TheOAsis is a multi-tenant SaaS platform built specifically for research supervision and academic workflow control.

It enables universities to manage:

- student and supervisor workflows
- proposal submission, review, and approval
- meeting logs and stage progression
- resource approvals and archive submissions
- analytics, audit trails, and management reporting

## Slide 6: How The Product Works

Students submit proposals, updates, meeting records, and archive materials through a structured workflow.

Supervisors review progress, approve or request changes, and maintain a clear supervision history.

Admins and super admins gain oversight into user activity, bottlenecks, risk signals, and institution-wide reporting.

This replaces fragmented academic operations with one traceable system of record.

## Slide 7: Why We Win

TheOAsis is not a generic portal. It is purpose-built for the supervision lifecycle.

Our product advantage comes from:

- workflow depth across student, supervisor, admin, and platform roles
- audit and analytics visibility for institutional control
- multi-tenant architecture for campus and multi-campus deployment
- commercial packaging suited to Nigerian university procurement

## Slide 8: Business Model

We use a B2B institutional SaaS model designed for higher-education procurement.

Revenue comes from three layers:

- annual campus subscription
- one-time implementation and onboarding
- premium add-ons for AI, reporting, integrations, SMS, and dedicated hosting

Current pricing ranges from ₦6 million to ₦24 million per year, with average first-year contract value of about ₦15.3 million per campus.

## Slide 9: Go-To-Market

We are pursuing a land-and-expand GTM motion.

Step one is to sell a paid pilot into a faculty, postgraduate school, or defined academic unit.

Step two is to prove value through faster approvals, better reporting, and cleaner record management.

Step three is to convert pilots into annual campus agreements and expand into additional faculties, schools, and add-on modules.
