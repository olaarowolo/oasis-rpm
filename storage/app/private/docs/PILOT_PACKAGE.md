# Pilot Package

## Objective

Run a paid pilot that proves TheOAsis can reduce supervision bottlenecks, improve visibility, and strengthen academic record keeping for one university unit.

## Recommended Pilot Scope

Choose one of the following:

- School of Postgraduate Studies
- one faculty with a high final-year project load
- thesis and project archive workflow for a defined unit

## Commercial Structure

- pilot fee: ₦1,500,000 to ₦3,000,000
- pilot duration: 8 to 12 weeks
- conversion credit: pilot fee credited toward full rollout if converted within 90 days

## Pilot Deliverables

- configured tenant environment
- institutional branding and access setup
- supervisor, student, and admin role configuration
- limited data migration or initial user import
- workflow setup for proposals, meetings, progress, and archive submissions
- administrator training and guided go-live
- end-of-pilot results review

## Success Metrics

- proposal turnaround time reduced by at least 30 percent
- at least 70 percent of target supervisors active on platform
- at least 70 percent of pilot students with updated progress records
- measurable reduction in manual follow-up and paper-based tracking
- clear reporting view for pilot administrators

## Pilot Timeline

### Week 1 to Week 2

- discovery and workflow mapping
- technical setup
- data import preparation

### Week 3 to Week 4

- configuration and user onboarding
- admin and supervisor training

### Week 5 to Week 8

- live pilot operations
- issue resolution
- metric tracking

### Week 9 to Week 12

- optional extended validation window
- executive review
- rollout proposal and expansion decision

## Exit Criteria

The pilot should end with one of three outcomes:

- convert to faculty-wide annual subscription
- expand to postgraduate school or institution-wide rollout
- defer expansion pending budget cycle and maintain proposal in pipeline

## Required Client Stakeholders

- executive sponsor
- operational owner
- ICT contact
- admin lead
- supervisor champion

## Recommended Next Step

Book a discovery workshop, define the pilot unit, and baseline current workflow metrics before pricing the full rollout.
