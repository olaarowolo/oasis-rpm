# TheOAsis Pricing Brochure

## Research Operations Infrastructure for Universities

TheOAsis helps universities digitize research supervision, project approvals, progress tracking, archive submission, and institutional reporting in one secure platform.

Designed for:

- Schools of Postgraduate Studies
- faculties running final-year projects
- research directorates
- ICT directorates
- institutional repositories and archives

## What The Platform Solves

- slow proposal and topic approval workflows
- limited visibility into supervision progress
- missing or fragmented audit records
- poor reporting for senate, management, and accreditation
- weak archive submission and retrieval processes
